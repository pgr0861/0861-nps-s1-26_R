Reflection

SSituation

I didn’t attend the video editing class or an external workshop. I learned video editing on my own, and used a video I edited for a group project in another unit, MTH0007.

TTask

The task was to record and edit a short video of about a minute, using techniques such as cutting clips together, adding images with pan or zoom, and adding a voiceover or sound effects.

AAction

I did all of the editing myself in DaVinci Resolve: cutting clips together, adding still images with pan and zoom, recording a voiceover, and adding music and on-screen text.

The trickiest part was getting effects to work in Fusion, DaVinci Resolve’s effects tool. I used AI to help me find and fix the errors I ran into.

RResult

I finished a video I edited entirely myself, and learned how to troubleshoot effects in Fusion when they didn’t work as expected.

What I’d do differently / next

I’ll use these skills in future group videos, for science communication, and in my own personal projects.
