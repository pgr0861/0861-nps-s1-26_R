    About me

    I’m a first-year astrophysics student at Swinburne, and I’d like to go into astrophysics research. Right now I’m focused on building my skills in programming and data analysis (see my professional purpose page).

    This unit was a core unit for me, so I went in with an open mind. I hoped to pick up practical skills, like spreadsheets, research and video, and to get experience working on a real problem with a group.

    My project

    Our group, Viridus Machina, asked whether algae could help shrink Swinburne’s carbon footprint. Swinburne still has some leftover emissions, such as the natural gas burned to heat its buildings, which it currently balances with carbon offsets. We looked at “insetting” instead: capturing carbon on campus by growing the algae Chlorella vulgaris in photobioreactors fed with CO2-rich exhaust from the campus boilers, then turning the algae into biogas that could go back into those boilers.

    My roles were data and analysis, including the solar exposure spreadsheet that showed Melbourne’s winter light is too low for the algae without LED lighting, editing our video, and writing parts of the script.
