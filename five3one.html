Task 5: Five3One

The Five3One group activity from Week 3, which I missed, and reflections on our group work across the semester.

About the activity

Five3One is a group activity played in Week 3, when we formed our project groups. It uses persona cards to explore the different roles and behaviours people bring to group work, and how those can affect a team.

I was absent on the day my group played it, so I didn’t take part, and I don’t have a record of the personas my group assigned. My reflections below are based on my own experience of working with my group across the project.

Group work check-in (Week 6/7)

At the Week 6/7 check-in, our group work was going well and we were on track. We had partly stuck to our group agreement.

The parts that slipped were meeting attendance, since not everyone made every meeting, and communication, with slow replies in our group chat. To finish successfully, we needed clearer roles and better communication.

Reflection: group work overall

SSituation

Our group, Viridus Machina, investigated whether algae grown in photobioreactors could help reduce Swinburne’s carbon footprint. My roles were data and analysis (including the solar exposure spreadsheet), editing our video, and writing parts of the script.

TTask

Together, we needed to research our idea and present it, including in a group video.

AAction

After the check-in, we divided tasks more clearly by person, and people took on the parts they were best at.

RResult

We produced a final result I’m happy with, and our communication improved over the semester. The workload wasn’t always even, though, with some people doing more than others.

I learned that communicating early saves problems later, that clear roles keep a group on track, and that groups work best when people do what they’re good at.

What I’d do differently / next

In my next group project, I’d keep the better communication we built up, but revisit our group agreement during the project rather than only at the start, and speak up sooner when I notice the workload becoming uneven.
